Software for education.

We build e-learning platforms, student management systems, and educational tools that transform how institutions deliver learning.

Nageye School of Languages

● Live

The client managed their English teaching business across multiple disconnected platforms — Zoom for live sessions, WhatsApp for student communication, Excel spreadsheets for financial tracking, email for file transfers, and a third-party platform for assessments. There was no student progress tracking, no unified dashboard, and critical data was scattered across five different tools. We built a single, unified e-learning platform that consolidates everything into one dashboard. Structured courses with interactive quizzes and assessments, personalised teacher feedback via voice notes, live video sessions powered by a self-hosted Jitsi server, subscription billing through Stripe, and comprehensive progress tracking and analytics for both students and instructors.
