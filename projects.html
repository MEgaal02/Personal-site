Nageye School of Languages

● Live VISIT LIVE SITE →

The Problem

The client was running an English teaching company using multiple disconnected tools across their daily operations:

  • Zoom for live lessons
  • WhatsApp for student communication
  • Excel spreadsheets for finance tracking
  • Email for file transfers and materials
  • A third-party platform for assessments
  • No student progress tracking at all

Our Plan

Design and build a single unified platform that consolidates every aspect of the business into one dashboard:

  • Custom e-learning platform with course delivery
  • Built-in assessment and quiz engine
  • Self-hosted video calling (replacing Zoom)
  • Teacher feedback system with voice notes
  • Stripe subscription billing (replacing manual invoicing)
  • Full student progress tracking and analytics

The Resolution

Delivered a production-grade, fully containerised platform deployed on AWS infrastructure:

  • Unified dashboard for teachers and students
  • All communication, lessons, and payments in one place
  • Self-hosted Jitsi video server for live sessions
  • Automated billing via Stripe subscriptions
  • S3 media storage with CloudFront CDN
  • SSL-secured with automated certificate renewal
