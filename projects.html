Nageye School of Languages

● Live

Nageye School of Languages is a full-featured e-learning platform designed for adult language learners. The platform delivers structured English courses with interactive quizzes, assessments, personalised teacher feedback via voice notes, and live video sessions for both group and 1-to-1 learning. It includes a subscription-based payment system, progress tracking, and analytics for students and instructors. We developed the complete full-stack solution, focusing on scalability, clean architecture, and an intuitive learning experience.

TaxGen AI

● Live

TaxGen AI is a collaborative, Chat Room-style platform designed to help users get top-tier support for their tax affairs. Users can create and join topic-based rooms to discuss tax issues in real time, share insights, and receive AI-assisted guidance.
