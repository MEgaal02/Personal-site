Nageye School of Languages

● Live VISIT LIVE SITE →

The Problem

The client ran an English teaching company across five disconnected tools with no unified dashboard or student progress tracking:

  • Zoom for live lessons
  • WhatsApp for student communication
  • Excel for finance tracking
  • Email for file transfers
  • Third-party platform for assessments
  • No progress tracking whatsoever

Our Plan

Design and build a single unified platform that consolidates every aspect of the business into one dashboard:

  • Custom e-learning platform with course delivery
  • Built-in assessment and quiz engine
  • Self-hosted video calling (replacing Zoom)

  • No real-time visibility into patrol coverage

Our Plan

Design and build a unified security operations management platform with supervisor and guard-facing interfaces:

Clutchly

● Live VISIT LIVE SITE →

The Problem

Getting a car repaired in the UK means garage queues, opaque pricing and no easy way to trust who's working on your car:

  • Days lost dropping cars at garages
  • Quotes that change once the bonnet is open
  • No way to vet independent mechanics
  • Skilled mobile mechanics with no platform to win work, take card payments or build a reputation

Our Plan

Build a two-sided marketplace that brings the mechanic to the driveway — with trust engineered into every step:

  • Booking platform connecting drivers with vetted mobile mechanics
  • AI-guided fault diagnosis before a mechanic is ever booked
  • Escrow payments held until the job is confirmed done
  • A trust system that rewards mechanics for consistent quality

The Resolution

Live across the UK as a co-founded venture, built and security tested in-house:

  • Stripe Connect escrow with a 48-hour satisfaction window
  • Trust tiers — New, Verified, Pro, Elite — driven by a six-component mechanic success score
  • Google Gemini AI diagnostics from plain-English symptom descriptions
  • Live mechanic tracking from booking to job completion
  • Free DVLA-backed Car Check reports driving organic signups
  • Fully serverless architecture on Vercel and Supabase

The Problem

Supported housing providers coordinate vulnerable residents, properties and compliance across disconnected tools:
